## About me

I'm an R&D Staff Engineer at Children's National Hospital in Washington, D.C., where I work on surgical navigation, robot-guided imaging, and AI/ML and LLM systems for healthcare.

My research interests include evaluation, post-training, recursive self-improvement, human-AI interaction, reinforcement learning, computer vision, and privacy and security.
